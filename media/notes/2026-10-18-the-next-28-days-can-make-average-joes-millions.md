## Key Terms
- **Fear & Greed Index:** A CNN sentiment gauge (0–100) measuring market emotion; readings below 25 indicate "Extreme Fear."
- **125-Day Moving Average:** A medium-term technical trendline used to gauge market momentum and mean-reversion levels.
- **Portfolio-Secured Put:** An options strategy of selling put options backed by total portfolio margin/equity rather than keeping 100% idle cash.
- **Intrinsic Value (IV):** The estimated true underlying value of a stock based on fundamental financial modeling, compared against its current market price.
- **Forward P/E Ratio:** The current stock or index price divided by projected earnings per share over the next 12 months.
- **Blended Earnings Growth:** A metric combining actual reported earnings from companies that have reported with consensus estimates for the remainder.
