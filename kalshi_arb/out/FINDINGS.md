# Kalshi sum-under-$1: findings

All numbers are an **upper bound**: top-of-book asks from 1-minute candles, no
depth. Generated by `kalshi_arb/scan.py`; per-run detail is in `last90/` and
`nba-playoffs-2026/`.

Every net > 0 minute was worth exactly +1c, except one at +3c. In a typical
minute the two asks summed to $1.01-$1.02.

## The 4 events, looked at by hand

- **NFL DAL-NYG, 2026-05-28 09:55 UTC** (3.5 months before kickoff). The
  asks were 0.59 + 0.36 = 0.95, +1c after fees. Trades printed at exactly
  those prices the next minute (19 contracts DAL, 4 NYG), so someone probably
  took it. Realistic size: about 4 contracts, about $0.04.
- **NFL CIN-PIT, 2026-09-15 18:06-18:13 UTC.** The market had been listed
  about 10 minutes earlier. The PIT ask flickered between 0.59 and 0.48, and
  nothing traded on either leg. Only 2 of the 7 minutes have real quotes on
  both legs; the other 5 are carried-forward quotes. This is a quoting
  artifact, not a fill.
- **NBA GSW-LAC, 2026-04-16 04:40 UTC** (gross 0.93, +3c) and **DEN-MIN,
  2026-05-01 04:08 UTC** (+1c). Both are in the last minutes of the game,
  with each side moving 20-35c a minute. Within each market the bid and ask
  were 1c apart, but the two markets disagreed: GSW bid 0.39 + LAC bid 0.52
  = 0.91. One leg's end-of-minute snapshot lagged the other by seconds. This
  is a timing artifact of the candle data. You couldn't have bought both
  asks at once.

## Bottom line

Across 296 games, the sum-under-$1 gap after taker fees essentially never
opened. When it did, it was worth one or two cents, lasted no longer than the
1-minute resolution can show, and was either a few contracts deep or an
artifact of comparing two books at slightly different instants.

## Not verified

- **Fee coefficients.** The 0.07 taker / 0.0175 maker rates and the rounding
  rule. The API confirms `quadratic_with_maker_fees`, multiplier 1,
  unchanged since 2026-01-01, but kalshi.com/fee-schedule is behind a bot
  checkpoint and docs.kalshi.com / assets.kalshi.com are blocked from the
  scan environment. Per-order rounding would make fees slightly lower at
  size.
- **Missing candles.** It's not confirmed that a missing 1-minute candle
  means the quote was unchanged. Quotes are carried forward for at most
  5 minutes, and those minutes are flagged.
- **Depth.** No historical order-book depth exists, so every ask is assumed
  fillable for one contract.
- **Postponement risk.** Every game's rules allow settlement "to a fair
  market price" if the game isn't played within 48 hours. No scanned game
  settled that way, but it's real risk to a "riskless" position.
- **Week labels.** The window selects games by close time, but weekly rows
  bucket by the minute the quote was seen. That's why DAL-NYG appears
  under 2026-W22.
